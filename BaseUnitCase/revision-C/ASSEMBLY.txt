REVISION C - BASE UNIT DESKTOP ENCLOSURE - 2026-10-11

Digital mechanical prototype completed; physical fit is UNTESTED.
Confirmed hardware: Pi 5 8GB WITHOUT cooler; nRF52840 DK revision 3.0.3,
user-reported additional board marking 2026.21. Spare cooler space is retained.
See CONFIRMED-HARDWARE-FIT.txt before printing in Bambu Studio.
User-authorized resumption supersedes the historical PAUSED status for this
revision only. Prototype A and the unfinished Revision B snapshot are preserved.
No firmware, Pi services, firewall, radio, Git identity, README or PCB was changed.

DESIGN
Official SC1635 7-inch Touch Display 2, landscape 1280 x 720. Screen is 15 degrees
BACK from vertical, 75 degrees above horizontal. This is not the old 45-degree
foot profile. Case 206.32 x 137.24 x 123 mm in display-local coordinates. Desk
support footprint 230.32 x 240 mm excluding screw-head projection. Screen top
177.56 mm above desk before pads; lower rear shell corner is 13.17 mm above desk.

Front bezel retains the outer glass perimeter using compliant tape. The Pi 5
8GB remains on the display's original supplied mounting arrangement. The radio
is inside the removable rear cover on three nylon-fastened towers. Side supports
bolt through the case wall into captive M4 nuts; no friction-fit stand tabs.

BUILD
Install tools OUTSIDE the repository. In your preferred Python virtual environment:
  python -m pip install cadquery==2.8.0 trimesh==5.1.1 pillow numpy
  python enclosure.py
  python verify_exports.py
  python render_assembly.py
Run commands in this revision-C directory. Nothing depends on a legacy workspace.
The rendering script uses Windows Segoe UI if available, with a default fallback.
Generated files are overwritten by their owning scripts. Edit the master first.

PRINT LIST / ORIENTATION
1 front_bezel: front face down, nominal 206.32 x 137.24 x 4 mm.
1 vented_body: retaining-ring/front face down, nominal 206.32 x 137.24 x 116 mm.
1 rear_cover_radio_mount: outside face down; towers up; 206.32 x 122.94 x 27 mm.
1 rear_cable_strip: outside face down; 206.32 x 14 x 3 mm.
2 side_stand_print_two: broad flat face down; 240 x 115.92 x 12 mm each.
1 fastener_coupon first: 45 x 24 x 8 mm; holes, insert bore, M4 nut recess.
The largest part needs a usable 240 x 116 mm area BEFORE brim and printer margins.
Allow about 250 mm usable length with a 5 mm brim at each end. User confirmed
Bambu Lab H2D Pro. Select its exact profile in Bambu Studio and inspect the plate
preview at 100% scale; no printer profile or slicing has been validated here.
Starting process proposal: PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers,
25-35% infill. Heavy solid-area side supports improve stability. Actual sliced
mass, bridging and strength must be checked. No slicer or print test was run.
Tall shell is open to the top in print orientation; vents bridge 3 mm. M4 holes
and nut recesses bridge locally; inspect sag and nut fit in slicer/coupon.

FASTENER / CONSUMABLE BOM (VERIFY COUPON BEFORE BUYING OR FULL PRINT)
4 M3 x 8 mm machine screws: front bezel, nominal 4 mm engagement after 4 mm bezel.
This replaces the earlier M3 x 10 recommendation and gives 2.1 mm blind-hole
bottom clearance. Do not use M3 x 10 without rechecking the actual stack.
6 M3 x 8 mm machine screws: rear cover (4) and lower strip (2), 5 mm engagement.
10 M3 heat-set inserts: 4 front / 6 rear. CAD has diameter 4.0 x 6.1 mm blind
pilot pockets. Select inserts with a supplier-specified pilot compatible with
this bore and length <= 5.5 mm, or edit the bore for your actual insert. Require
an effective threaded length >=4 mm. Keep outside diameter <=5 mm in the 8 mm
columns; an insert diameter is NOT its pilot-hole diameter. Do not
assume every M3 insert fits. Install with no electronics/glass in the enclosure.
4 M4 x 25 mm low-profile/button-head screws plus 4 M4 hex nuts: two per stand.
M4 nut pockets: 7.2 mm across flats, 3.4 mm effective depth, accessible inside.
Nominal standard M4 nut 7 mm AF / 3.2 mm thick; coupon confirms print tolerance.
With 12 mm stand and 12 mm wall boss, screw tips extend approximately 1 mm inward.
3 M3 x 35 mm NYLON screws, 3 nylon nuts and 6 thin nylon washers for the DK.
Rear plate 3 + tower 24 + PCB assumed 1.6 + washers/nut leave a small thread tail.
Use 6 mm or smaller washer OD at PCB; verify each real board landing zone.
Place one nylon washer under each external screw head and one under each
component-side nut. NO washer between PCB underside and the support tower;
adding one changes the stated board plane and clearances.
Reuse original supplied display/Pi M2.5 screws/standoffs. No replacement screw
length is specified for display threads. Do not bottom longer screws into it.
Nonconductive compliant tape, nominal 0.5 mm front + 0.5 mm rear of glass edge.
Non-slip desk pads for both runners and small nylon cable ties for strain relief.
Fastener preload, material strength and insert pull-out have not been qualified.

ASSEMBLY / SERVICE (UNPOWERED HARDWARE AT A USER-PLANNED MAINTENANCE TIME)
1. Print coupon; check screw clearance, nut fit and chosen heat-set insert.
   Install body inserts and inspect for bulging, splits or melt intrusion.
2. Lay bezel front-down on a clean soft support. Place compliant tape only on
   straight outer-glass contact edges. Insert glass into 190.32 x 121.24 pocket.
   Nominal side clearance is 0.5 mm per side. Aperture is 184.32 x 115.24 mm;
   it does not overlap the active 154.56 x 86.94 display area.
3. Attach bare Pi to display with original hardware. The verified mounting pose
   has USB-A/Ethernet facing case right and USB-C facing the lower case edge.
   A reversed Pi pose has not been checked for plugged-cable access. Keep FFC
   out of the lip and screws. Pi and any future added stack must stay inside the envelope
   in SOURCES.txt; no HAT/SSD compatibility has been established.
4. Add rear-edge compliant tape. Lower body onto bezel. Body must bottom on the
   bezel shoulder, never clamp the glass. Pocket height 2.2 mm versus nominal
   0.7 glass + 0.5 + 0.5 tape = 1.7 mm. Adjust soft tape to remove rattle without
   glass bending. Tighten four bezel screws gently with frame supported.
5. Insert four M4 nuts from inside the open rear into side pockets. Fit two stands
   outside the shell and fasten with M4 screws. Both runners must sit flat. Add
   non-slip pads at both front and rear ends; verify equal pad thickness.
6. The user confirmed nRF52840 DK 3.0.3; the CAD matches its official three
   mounting centres. Check printed fit without force. Board underside faces
   cover; components face the display. Place on the three 24 mm towers and fasten
   with nylon screws/washers/nuts. Never pull PCB down over an underside component.
   Official laminate specification says nominal ~1.6 mm. Check actual component
   landing zones; the drawings do not certify your printed washers/tower fit.
7. Connect the same radio USB port already used by the firmware: J2 is interface-
   MCU USB at the left edge; J3 is nRF USB at the lower edge. Do not swap ports
   assuming equivalent operation. For either route, reserve a plug projection
   <=25 mm plus approximately 10 mm for its bend. The old 35 mm straight-space
   figure did not also provide a bend; longer boots may need right-angle cables.
   Verify the actual cable and leave a loose service loop. Preserve a gentle FFC bend (initial 10 mm radius allowance only;
   actual cable specification governs). Keep all cables left/below the antenna
   region. Rear-cover removal needs a disconnectable or sufficiently slack loop.
8. Keep the DK-to-Pi USB cable entirely inside, with a removable-cover service
   loop below/left of the antenna reserve. Only the Pi power supply lead exits.
   Fit rear cover using four M3 screws. Put the insulated Pi power cable in the
   single 24 x 12 mm split exit, then fit lower strip with two M3 screws. The
   0.3 mm horizontal seam gives print clearance. Deburr edges; use a soft sleeve
   or edge protection. Tie slots provide strain relief on insulated cable only.
9. Case is enclosed except ventilation and cable apertures; it is NOT sealed,
   dustproof, spill-proof or an ingress-protection-rated electrical enclosure.
10. For servicing, shut down/disconnect power, support cover, release strain-relief
    ties as needed and remove four cover screws. Withdraw cover rearward without
    pulling USB. The lower strip can stay or be removed to release cables without
    threading plugs. Access to Pi ports/SD and DK switches requires cover removal.
    Remove the body only after supporting the glass/Pi assembly on a soft surface.

DIGITAL VALIDATION / LIMITS
All six unique printable parts pass OpenCascade B-rep validity, one-solid checks,
closed/wound STL topology, positive volume and independent STEP/STL volume agreement.
Six assembled structural solids have zero pairwise volumetric collision. The
75-degree plane and backward lean are measured again from the exported STEP.
Hardware reserves clear the shell; DK underside clears it except intended towers.
Rear cover/board extraction checked at offsets 5, 15, 35, 60 and 100 mm; this is
sampled solid clearance, not proof of every cable sweep or every intermediate pose.
Bare-Pi mass model assumes 45% effective solid PETG + display 300 g + bare Pi
50 g (estimate) + DK 45 g + wires/fasteners 50 g. Current exact estimated mass,
COM and tip threshold are in validation.json; none are measured.
Predicted tip threshold is approximately 5.5 N at screen-top height;
actual print mass, pads, friction, tapping, cable pull and loading can change it.
Use validation.json for exact current numbers. This is not a measured force rating.
