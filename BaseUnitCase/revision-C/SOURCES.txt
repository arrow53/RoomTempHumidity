COORDINATES AND CLEARANCE CONTRACT
Display-local x right, y up, z into rear. Front bezel plane z=0, body starts z=4,
rear cover inner plane z=120, outer plane z=123. Desk transform:
X=x; Y=30+cos(75deg)*y+sin(75deg)*z;
Z=45+sin(75deg)*y-cos(75deg)*z.
The top edge moves rearward as height increases. This explicitly replaces 45deg.

Display glass x8.5..197.82, y8.5..128.74, z2.3..3.0.
Rear display reserve x15.84..190.98, y18.265..117.975, z3.0..17.22.
Pi/cooler reserve x38..165, y30..110, z17.22..55. This deliberately exceeds bare
Pi footprint; it is not an assertion of an exact installed pose or all plug fit.
No HAT or SSD allowance is promised. Actual hardware must fit entirely inside it.
A separate 25 mm-wide left cable routing lane is reserved x13..38, y35..105,
z25..63; local connector clearance and cable routing still need a real dry fit.
Radio PCB lower-left x42,y40, z94.4..96.0. Components toward display: reserve
20 mm, z74.4..94.4. Underside toward cover: reserve 20 mm, z96..116, with 4 mm
additional space to cover apart from intended support towers. Towers 24 mm.
Old Pi spare-envelope to radio component separation remains 19.4 mm. With
the official bare Pi model at the documented mounting pose, actual model upper
extent is z34.716 and separation to the radio reserve is 39.684 mm. There is no
fan installed. Ventilation geometry does not establish passive thermal adequacy.
Radio left USB total service reserve 35 mm, x7..42, y62..86, z77..95. For a
turning cable this must be apportioned: <=25 mm projecting plug + ~10 mm bend
allowance. J3 lower-edge route is now checked separately; see the fit report.
Antenna-side keep-clear zone x157..178.525, y40..103.5, z74.4..116. It is a
conservative last-21.525-mm board-region reservation, not a Nordic RF guarantee.
No printed towers or added fasteners inside that zone. Keep user cables/metal
outside it. Display-metal depth separation >=57.18 mm; PETG and rear vent ribs
remain nearby, so RF must be checked in the actual enclosure. Do not paint the
case with metallic/conductive coatings. DK components themselves are not removed
from this reserved area. Our STEP proxies overlap one another intentionally;
they are explanatory envelopes, not independent physical parts.

Display drawing: portrait hole x=35.62/84.62 (49 span), y=75.34/133.34 (58 span).
After landscape rotation and glass origin (8.5,8.5), mounting centres are

verify actual mounting orientation during dry fit. No Pi/display solid-vs-solid
collision claim is made because only the Pi has a detailed vendor STEP here.

J3 (89.7293,4.9311) mm, facing down in this board-local orientation. Both are
MicroUSB-B. Top and bottom assembly drawings inspected: battery holder is
on underside; selected hole circles avoid its outline. Washer/tower contact
zones visually clear, but exact component envelopes and solder fillets were
not extracted; inspect real landing zones before tightening. No claim of exact
3D component clearance on the DK. Hardware user confirmation does not remove
the cable, inserts, print tolerance, thermal/RF or mechanical load-test gates.
