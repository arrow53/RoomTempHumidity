PORTS, SWITCHES AND SERVICE
Pi preferred orientation: USB-A/RJ45 to case right, USB-C toward case bottom.
Use original display standoffs/screws. Reversed orientation is not plug-validated.
DK J2 at left is interface-MCU USB. J3 at bottom is nRF USB. Retain the working
firmware's actual connection; this design does not make the ports interchangeable.
The DK-to-Pi USB cable stays entirely internal. Only Pi power uses the single
split lower exit. Leave an internal USB service loop for rear-cover removal. Use short
boots: <=25 mm projection beyond board-edge/socket-mouth region plus ~10 mm for
turning, within the illustrated reserves. Actual cable bend specification governs;
longer or stiff boots may require right-angle cables. Do not treat the previous
35 mm straight allowance as 35 mm of plug PLUS a bend.
The DK buttons/reset/selectors and Pi power button/SD/ports have NO external
actuators or custom port cutouts. Access requires removing the rear cover. DK
controls face inward, so withdraw/support the cover before reaching them. USB
service loops must allow it to move; disconnect first if they do not. Power down
and unplug for assembly. Pi SD access can be constrained by the display and may
need loosening the original mount; do not claim tool-free card servicing.

FASTENER CLARIFICATION / ACTUAL PRINT BLOCKERS
1. Exact M3 insert maker/part number is still needed. Current bore is diameter
   4.0 mm x 6.1 mm effective blind depth. Specify supplier-recommended 4.0 mm
   pilot, insert length <=5.5 mm, useful thread >=4 mm and OD <=5 mm. An M3 label
   alone is not sufficient. The common 5.7 mm-length insert does NOT meet this
   current specification; do not force it in. Adjust bore/depth if your insert
   differs, then rerun checks. Print/test fastener_coupon first.
2. Bezel screw correction: use 4 x M3x8, rather than earlier M3x10. It gives
   nominal 4 mm engagement and 2.1 mm bottom clearance. Rear stays 6 x M3x8,
   giving 5 mm engagement and 1.1 mm bottom clearance. No extra washers are
   assumed in these dimensions. Do not overtighten against the glass.
3. Stand fasteners unchanged: 4 x M4x25 and 4 M4 nuts (7 mm AF nominal); pockets
   7.2 mm AF x 3.4 mm deep. DK: 3 x M3x35 nylon screws/nuts, six thin nylon
   washers <=6 mm OD. Put washers under external screw heads and component-side
   nuts, NEVER between PCB and support tower. Verify real component landing zones.
4. Printer confirmed by user: Bambu Lab H2D Pro. Select that exact profile in
   Bambu Studio and inspect the plate preview. No slicer verification performed.
   Longest part 240 x 115.92 x 12 mm; about 250 mm length needed with a 5 mm brim.
   Tall body 206.32 x 137.24 x 116 mm. Keep all parts at their original scale.
   Import STL at 100% / millimetres. Do not scale down to fit the plate.
5. Confirm actual Pi USB-C and chosen DK USB cable boot length/width/bend radius,
   then unpowered dry fit. The digital boxes cannot qualify unspecified cables.
