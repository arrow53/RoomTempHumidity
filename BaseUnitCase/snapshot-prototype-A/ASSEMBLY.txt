SC1635 / Raspberry Pi 5 / PCA10056 desktop enclosure prototype
Revision A, 2026-10-10. Display face is 45 degrees to the desk.

STATUS
Editable CAD and closed printable meshes are supplied. This is an open-back
enclosure/stand prototype, not a physically validated production enclosure.
User confirmed SC1635 and requested 45 degrees. Landscape is assumed.
Radio assumed nRF52840 DK (PCA10056); exact PCB revision is still required.
Cooler model, printer bed, and actual cable plugs remain unconfirmed.
No running device, firmware, dashboard, or system configuration was changed.

FILES / EDITING
enclosure.py is the parametric master. Change parameters and run with Python 3
to regenerate meshes, enclosure.scad and mesh-checks.json; no packages needed.
The foot profile is explicitly constructed for 45 degrees. Changing ANGLE alone
does NOT create different feet; redesign their polygon for another angle.
enclosure.scad is editable OpenSCAD CSG. Set part="assembly" for the assembled
preview, or select a named part. Python regenerating overwrites this SCAD file.
STL dimensions are millimetres; import into the slicer at 100% scale.

PRINT LIST AND ORIENTATION
1 x frame.stl: front face on bed, tabs included. 206.32 x 153.24 x 6 mm.
1 x retainer_bottom.stl and 1 x retainer_top.stl: flat on bed.
2 x foot_45_print_two.stl: broad side on bed, 195 x 45 x 12.6 mm each.
1 x radio_dock.stl: base on bed, towers upward, 86 x 69.5 x 15 mm.
1 x fit_coupon.stl first: test 6.6 mm channel and 3.4 mm square screw hole.
The square holes are intentional printable clearances, not threads.
Recommended starting settings: PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom
layers, 25-35% infill. No intended supports. Inspect slicer layer preview.
The frame needs a usable area at least 207 x 154 mm before brim or margins.
A 220 x 220 mm bed is assumed, not confirmed. Do not scale to fit a small bed.
Retainer span is also 206.32 mm. If bed is smaller, redesign split joints.

PROPOSED FASTENER BOM (ONLY FASTENERS)
4 x M3 x 12 mm machine screws, 4 x M3 nuts, 8 x M3 flat washers:
  attach the two removable retainers to the frame, outside the glass outline.
3 x M3 x 22 mm nylon machine screws, 3 x M3 nylon nuts,
  6 x M3 nylon flat washers: attach DK to three printed towers.
Reuse the original supplied M2.5 screws to attach Pi to display standoffs.
Check engagement before tightening. No new display screw length is assumed.
2 x small nylon cable ties for radio dock strain relief.
Do not substitute long screws in the display's threaded standoffs.

ASSEMBLY
1. Print the coupon and check a 6 mm tab/flat sample in its 6.6 mm channel.
   Check an M3 screw in the square hole. Adjust SLOT_CLEARANCE if required.
2. Inspect/deburr the frame. Pad the FRONT glass-support lip and rear retainer
   contact zones with thin nonconductive compliant tape, initially 0.5 mm each.
   Padding and optional non-slip feet are assembly consumables, not in the
   fastener BOM. Bare printed plastic must not press against the glass.
3. At a planned maintenance shutdown, disconnect power before handling the Pi.
   Preserve the current display/Pi wiring. Use the supplied display standoffs
   and screws for the Pi; do not transfer its load to the ribbon cable.
4. Lay the frame front down on a soft clean surface. Place display glass into
   its 190.32 x 121.24 mm pocket. The front lip overlaps 3 mm at straight edges.
   Do not force it. Glass corners are R8; this pocket deliberately does not
   follow the corners and must support the straight bezel edges evenly.
5. Fit bottom retainer at local y=0 and top at y=126.24, both starting z=4.
   Tighten only until the retainer seats against the FRAME. Screws must never
   clamp the glass. Front padding + glass + rear padding must fit in the
   2.2 mm pocket depth (z=1.8 to 4); nominal 0.5+0.7+0.5=1.7 mm.
   If glass rattles, adjust compliant padding, not screw force. Check that
   rear retainers contact only the outer 3 mm of glass and no metalwork.
6. Orient both feet with their long tails behind the display. Slide the two
   6 mm-thick lower tabs into the slanted 6.6 mm slots. There is 16 mm tab
   engagement in a 16.4 mm-deep slot. Feet are removable gravity-fit parts;
   lift the assembly by its frame, not by one foot. Add non-slip underside pads.
7. Mount radio using the three holes on the debugger half/centre, with USB
   debugger connector to the left and antenna/SoC end overhanging the right.
   Board lower-left is dock (3,3). Towers support PCB underside at z=15.
   Check underside battery holder and solder tails have clearance FIRST.
   Never pull the PCB down over an underside component with the mounting screws.
8. Keep the radio separate, antenna end toward free space. Start at least
   50 mm from display metalwork, Pi, cables and metal desk objects. This is a
   conservative layout target, NOT a Nordic guaranteed RF clearance rule.
   Use nonmetal fasteners. No printed material extends under the antenna end.
9. Use dock tie slots for USB strain relief: capture the insulated cable with
   a loose service loop, never a connector or PCB. Secure Pi power cable to a
   foot using a removable tie around its body, without covering the SD/ports.
   Keep a pull-free service loop. No cables should carry the assembly weight.

CLEARANCE / ENGINEERING CHECKS
Verified from official drawing: 7-inch glass outline 189.32 x 120.24, R8;
0.7 mm lens, rear assembly up to 14.92 mm. Viewing aperture is 155 x 88.
Prototype front opening 184.32 x 115.24; centred aperture has approximately
14.66 and 13.62 mm spare per side. Retainers overlap only 3 mm of outer bezel.
Display's separate mounting features: 49 x 58 mm Pi pattern, external ears
70.71 x 140 mm. This prototype uses the glass perimeter and original Pi
standoffs; it does not depend on uncertain ear-thread engagement or offsets.

Pi official drawing: 85 mm board length, 58 x 49 mm mounting centres,
diameter 2.7 mm mounting holes. The drawing is explicitly approximate.
Official active cooler drawing: 63.5 x 42.5 mm footprint, 13.7 mm height.
Actual installed cooler unconfirmed. Rear, sides and top are open: no close
wall or fan grille limits that envelope. Allow at least 10 mm free air beyond
actual fan inlet and fin outlets. No thermal qualification has been performed.

DK 3.0.3 official Gerber outline: x=0..136.525 mm including right projection,
y=0..63.5 mm. Main rectangular body is 133.985 mm long; do not confuse the two.
Drill tool T08 is 0.1260 in = 3.2004 mm. Selected hole centres, from Gerber
origin, are (13.97,7.62), (15.24,55.88), (66.04,12.70) mm.
Other T08 holes exist; only these three are used. Towers are 8 mm square with
3.4 mm square holes; verify washer clearance against actual nearby parts.
Dock ends at board x=83 mm, leaving roughly 53.5 mm of right board unsupported.
The radio remains accessible for switches, both USB ports, buttons, reset,
debug headers and battery inspection. It is not intended for pressing hard
on the overhanging PCB or attaching shields without extra supports.

CONNECTORS AND CABLES
Open rear and sides avoid revision-specific port cutouts. Verify actual Pi
USB-C, USB-A, Ethernet, micro-HDMI, GPIO, fan lead and microSD can be reached
with the frame and feet in place. Frame's perimeter retainers may require
moving cable routes; do not thread cables under a retainer or pinch a latch.
Reserve an initial 35 mm straight plug/service region at USB-C/USB-A and DK
USB connectors, and >=10 mm gentle FFC bend radius as prototype layout
allowances. These are not published cable minima. Actual cable specification
and connector boot geometry take precedence. Do not crease or strain the FFC.
Because cables exit into open space, longer/large plugs can project beyond
the stand. Confirm tabletop contact and routing at the 45-degree angle.

VALIDATION COMPLETED
All six mesh files: every undirected triangle edge has exactly two incidences,
consistent opposite winding, positive enclosed volume, and documented bounds.
Geometry is generated from exact box boundaries and an extruded foot polygon.
No CAD kernel, slicer, physical print, dimensional test, load/tip test, RF range
test, thermal soak, or installed-cable test has been performed.

REQUIRED BEFORE FULL PRINT / INSTALL
- Confirm radio marking is PCA10056 and provide its printed revision.
- Confirm cooler model, any HAT or SSD, and printer usable bed dimensions.
- Check landscape display orientation is acceptable.
- Measure glass dimensions/edge stack and test padded perimeter fit.
- Print coupon and inspect slicer preview at true scale.
- Compare three DK hole centres using a paper template or low-infill dock.
- Check battery holder clearance above plate and clear tower landing zones.
- Dry assemble unpowered; check glass stress, retainers, all plugs and SD access.
- Check cable pull and fingertip taps do not slide or tip the stand.
- After installation, check real workload temperature and radio link quality.
